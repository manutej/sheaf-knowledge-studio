⊘ Counterpoint · Non-Gluing Covers & Cohomological Obstructions (H¹ ≠ 0)
Not every selection of stalks U = {Ui} automatically glues into a single connected global section. If you select two distant stalks (for example, Scientific Agreement in Ch 1 and Universal Colimits in Ch 6) without including a bridging stalk in Ui ∩ Uj (such as Ologs or Categories & Functors), the restriction audit flags a Separated Cover (ρ = 0 on the missing overlap) and prompts you to add the mediating stalk rather than hallucinating an unsupported link.

⊘ Counterpoint · Directional Reading Trap (g ˆ f vs. Path Order)
Beginners frequently confuse algebraic composition order g ˆ f (read right-to-left: "apply f first, then g") with diagrammatic path order X →f Y →g Z (drawn left-to-right). ⊘ Counterpoint · Avoiding Purely Decorative "Visual Math"
Many visual category theory explainers draw pretty nodes and arrows but leave the reader unable to read or write a real proof or database migration equation. Our course enforces bi-directional manipulation: every time a learner drags an arrow in the diagram, the symbolic equation updates; every time they click a sub-term in HomD(L(c),d) ≅ HomC(c,R(d)), the corresponding morphism highlights and evaluates on concrete data.
