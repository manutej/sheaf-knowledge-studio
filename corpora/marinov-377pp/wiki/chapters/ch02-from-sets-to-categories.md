# Chapter 2: Categories — External Definitions, Products, Coproducts & Duality

**Page Span:** `pp. 52–94`

**Theme:** Shifting from internal set elements to external point-free arrows; universal products (A × B), categorical duality (reversing arrows), coproducts (A + B), initial/terminal objects, and the formal definition of a category.

## Global Invariants

- External Point-Free Principle (p. 52, p. 82): Objects are opaque points; all structure is encoded in morphisms and their composition.
- Universal Product & Coproduct Duality (p. 69): Reversing all arrows in the Product cone (π₁, π₂, ⟨f,g⟩) yields the Coproduct cocone (ι₁, ι₂, [f,g]).
- Category Laws (p. 82, p. 92): Associativity h ∘ (g ∘ f) = (h ∘ g) ∘ f and Identity id_B ∘ f = f = f ∘ id_A.

## Concept Stalks in this Chapter

- [From Internal Pairs to External Categorical Products (A × B)](../concepts/external-products-and-projections.md) (`§2.1`, `pp. 52–68`): Constructing Cartesian products A × B first internally as grids of pairs (a,b), then externally (point-free) purely via projection morphisms π₁ : A × B → A, π₂ : A × B → B and the universal mediating arrow ⟨f, g⟩.
- [Categorical Duality, Coproducts (A + B) & Initial/Terminal Objects](../concepts/categorical-duality-and-coproducts.md) (`§2.2`, `pp. 69–81`): The principle of Categorical Duality: reversing every arrow in the external diagram of Product yields Coproduct (disjoint union / sum A + B with injections ι₁, ι₂ and co-pairing [f, g]), and reversing Terminal Object 1 yields Initial Object 0.
- [The Formal Definition of a Category & Point-Free Epistemology](../concepts/categories-objects-morphisms-laws.md) (`§2.3`, `pp. 82–94`): The Rosetta dictionary between Set Theory (internal elements) and Category Theory (external morphisms); objects, morphisms, composition (∘), identity arrows (id_A), commutative diagrams, and why associativity + identity are the exact laws of composition.
