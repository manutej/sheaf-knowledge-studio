# Monoids, Agent Actions & Finite State Machines (`(M, e, ⋆) ↷ S`)

> **Sheaf Stalk Location:** Chapter 4 (Categories and Functors, Without Admitting It: Monoids, Groups, Graphs, Orders & Databases) · Section `4.1` · **PDF Pages 160–191**

## 1. Epistemic Worldview & Intuition

A monoid (M, e, *) is a set equipped with an associative binary operation and unit element—equivalent to a category with a single object. A monoid action M × S -> S models how sequences of commands, events, or time-steps deterministically transition the state space S of an agent or finite state machine.

## 2. Formal Categorical Core

Monoid laws (Def 4.1.1.1, p. 164): (m_1 * m_2) * m_3 = m_1 * (m_2 * m_3) and e * m = m = m * e. Free monoid List(Sigma) generates all finite strings over alphabet Sigma. Monoid action alpha : M × S -> S satisfies (e . s) = s and (m_1 * m_2) . s = m_1 . (m_2 . s) (Def 4.1.2.1, p. 173).

### `Definition 4.1.1.1` (Monoid) — [PDF Page 164]
> Definition 4.1.1.1 (Monoid). A monoid is a sequence (M, e, ⋆), where M is a set, e ∈ M is an element, and ⋆: M × M → M is a function, such that the following monoid laws hold for all m, n, p ∈ M: m ⋆ e = m. e ⋆ m = m. (m ⋆ n) ⋆ p = m ⋆ (n ⋆ p). We refer to e as the unit element and to ⋆ as the multiplication formula for the monoid.1 We call the first two rules unit laws and the third rule the associativity law for monoids.

### `Slogan 4.1.0.14` — [PDF Page 162]
> Slogan 4.1.0.14. The performance of a sequence of several actions is itself the performance of an action—a more complex action, but an action nonetheless. Mathematical objects called monoids and groups are tasked with encoding the agent’s perspective, i.e., what the agent can do, and what happens when she does a sequence of actions in succession. A monoid can be construed as a set of actions together with a formula that encodes how a sequence of actions is itself considered an action. A group is the same as a monoid except that every action is required to be reversible.
