# Equivalence Relations, Pushouts & Gluing in Set (`X ⊔_W Y`)

> **Sheaf Stalk Location:** Chapter 3 (Fundamental Considerations in Set: Limits, Colimits & Universal Constructions) · Section `3.3` · **PDF Pages 110–127**

## 1. Epistemic Worldview & Intuition

Given a span X <--f-- W --g--> Y representing an overlap region W mapped into both X and Y, the pushout X ⊔_W Y glues X and Y together by taking their disjoint union X ⊔ Y and quotienting by the equivalence relation f(w) ~ g(w) for all w in W.

## 2. Formal Categorical Core

X ⊔_W Y = (X ⊔ Y) / ~, where ~ is the equivalence relation generated by i_1(f(w)) ~ i_2(g(w)) for all w in W (Def 3.3.2.1, p. 118; Prop 3.3.2.8, p. 122). Dual to pullback: pasting two pushout squares yields a pushout rectangle.

- **`[Y]` $\xrightarrow{\text{glues via i₂ into}}$ `[P]`**

### Commutative Path Facts ($p \simeq q$)
- Commutative Gluing Square: i₁ ∘ f = i₂ ∘ g (Definition 3.3.2.1, p. 118).
- Universal Property (Prop 3.3.2.8, p. 122): Any pair of maps X -> Z and Y -> Z that agree on the overlap W glue uniquely to a map X ⊔_W Y -> Z.
